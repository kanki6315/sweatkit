Privacy policy

SweatKit is made by Arjuna Kankipati. Your activity library is stored on your device. No SweatKit account is required.

With your permission, SweatKit reads workouts, routes, and related health and fitness data from Apple Health or Garmin, or imports files you choose. This data is used to display and analyze your activities, never for advertising or sale.

Connections contact their respective providers; maps use Apple services. Those services have their own privacy policies. SweatKit does not upload your activity library to a developer-operated server or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

You can revoke Apple Health access in iOS settings and permanently delete activities in SweatKit. To stop Garmin access, revoke your API key through Garmin. Deleting an activity in SweatKit does not delete its source in Apple Health or Garmin. Device backups may retain eligible app data under your Apple backup settings.

This website uses no cookies or analytics. Cloudflare hosts it and may process request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. If you email support, we use your message and email address to respond; contact us to request deletion.

Privacy questions: sweatkit@arjunakankipati.com.
